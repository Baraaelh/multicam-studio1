Privacy Policy

Multicam Studio · Developed by Baraa Alhaddad · Last updated: October 28, 2026

Multicam Studio turns several Android phones on the same Wi-Fi network into a multi-camera production system. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Videos and audio

Camera and microphone access is used only to preview, record and stream video and audio during a session. Live previews travel directly between phones over your local network. Recordings are saved on the phone that made them and copied to the director's phone over the same local network. Videos and audio are never uploaded to our servers or any cloud service.

Data stored in the cloud

To let phones find each other, the app uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) to exchange a small amount of session data:

  • the 6-digit session code and session status;
  • connection setup messages (including local network addresses) needed to link the phones;
  • each camera's device model name (for example "Samsung Galaxy S23"), shown to the director;
  • recording commands and file information such as duration and size (not the files themselves).

This data is deleted when the director ends the session. Session codes expire after 24 hours.

Sign-in and app integrity

The app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. No name, email, phone number or account is required, and the anonymous ID is not linked to your identity. Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) is used to confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties, other than Google acting as our cloud provider.
  • No access to photos, videos or files created by other apps.

Your choices

You can revoke camera or microphone permission at any time in Android settings. Recordings can be deleted from your phone's gallery or file manager. Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and shown in the app.
